- **Sector:** Yacht Charters
- **Location:** Marina Plaza, Suite #2906, Dubai Marina
- **Website:** https://www.championyachts.ae/
- **Contact:** info@championyachts.ae (verified via Facebook page & site footer)
- **Instagram:** @championyachtsdubai
- **Demo to use:** https://celeste-events.vercel.app (luxury event/booking experience)
- **Pain points:**
  1. **SEO-stuffed, repetitive copy** — homepage is a wall of keyword-stuffed text ("yacht rental Dubai" repeated 15+ times). Reads like it was written for Google, not humans. Zero visual storytelling for a luxury brand.
  2. **No real online booking flow** — contact page is just a bare form ("enter your email and phone number"). For a service that starts at hundreds of AED, there's no fleet gallery with filters, no availability checker, no package comparison.
  3. **Inconsistent brand identity** — "About Us" says founded in 2003, then 2009 in the same section. Copy has grammatical errors. The site uses a basic WordPress theme with no custom design — doesn't match the luxury positioning at all.
- **Priority:** High

### 2. Mestaria Gallery
- **Sector:** Art Gallery (Contemporary)
- **Location:** Al Khawaneej, Dubai
- **Website:** https://mestaria.art/
- **Contact:** hello@mestaria.art (verified on contact page)
- **Instagram:** @mestariagallery
- **Demo to use:** https://lumina-lens-six.vercel.app (visual portfolio showcase)
- **Pain points:**
  1. **Homepage shows almost no art** — for a gallery, the homepage is shockingly empty. It's a Squarespace site with a couple of links and a mailing list signup. Visitors can't browse exhibitions or artists without clicking deep into subpages.
  2. **Ticketing is outsourced to Ticket Tailor** — redirects visitors away from the site for event bookings, breaking the brand experience and losing potential data/conversions.
  3. **No e-commerce or art inquiry system** — no way to browse available works, request pricing, or express interest in pieces online. For a gallery competing in Dubai's art scene, this is a missed revenue channel.
- **Priority:** High

- **Website:** https://cmadubai.com/
- **Contact:** enquiries@cmadubai.com (verified via department contact page); Founder: talabadri@cmadubai.com
- **Instagram:** @cmadubai (likely)
- **Demo to use:** https://solara-music-chi.vercel.app (music/creative industry)
- **Pain points:**
  1. **Dated, blog-style layout** — the homepage is just a list of "Read more" cards (Introduction, Exam Info, Term Dates, Fees). No hero section, no imagery of students/performances, no emotional hook. Looks like a CMS from 2012.
  2. **No online enrollment or trial booking** — parents must email or call to register. There's no class schedule viewer, no "book a trial lesson" flow, no student portal. Competitors like Melodica and Brooklyn Melodies have more modern funnels.
  3. **Information is fragmented** — fees, term dates, exam info, and contact details are all on separate pages requiring multiple clicks. A parent researching music lessons has to hunt for basic info instead of getting a clear overview.
- **Priority:** High
