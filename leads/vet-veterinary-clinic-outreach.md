# Vet Veterinary Clinic — Outreach Package

## Website Analysis

**Current site:** vetveterinaryclinic.com (redirects to vetclinicuae.com)
**Platform:** Shopify
**Focus:** Pet food e-commerce (Royal Canin, Trovet, Calibra, Josera prescription diets)

### Findings:
1. **Site is a pet food store, not a clinic website.** The homepage is entirely product-focused — prescription diets, "60 min delivery in Dubai," product collections. A pet owner searching for a vet in Al Barsha lands on what looks like an online pet food shop, not a veterinary clinic.

2. **Services page is broken/empty.** The "Vet Services" page contains zero service descriptions — just a repeating emergency phone number banner ("For any Emergencies please call 050-102-5092") duplicated 12+ times. No service list, no pricing, no information about what the clinic actually does.

3. **No booking, no WhatsApp, no Google Maps.** There is no way to book an appointment online. No WhatsApp click-to-chat (standard in Dubai). No embedded map to help people find the clinic. The only contact method is a phone number buried in a repeating banner.

### Tone/positioning:
- "We Care More" — compassionate, warm, pet-focused
- Emphasize 5+ years in business, 30 years veterinary experience
- Products: Trovet, Royal Canin, Hills Diet, Calibra, Josera
