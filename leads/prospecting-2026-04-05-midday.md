# Vertical: Accounting / Tax Advisory (Dubai)

Why this vertical: UAE's 9% corporate tax went live in 2023 and FTA compliance deadlines are hitting SMEs hard through 2025–2026. There's a wave of small-to-mid boutique tax firms that set up to capture this demand — but the vast majority have websites that look like DIY WordPress sites from 2018: stock photos of calculators, generic service lists, no trust signals, no personality. The demand for credible digital presence in this vertical is *right now* because clients Google them before calling, and the first impression determines whether they get the enquiry. Big opportunity: these firms charge AED 5,000–30,000/month retainers, so a quality website is an obvious ROI. We haven't done this vertical. Perfect timing.
