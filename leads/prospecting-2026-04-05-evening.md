# Vertical: Wedding / Event Videography
Why: Dubai's luxury wedding market (destination weddings, Arabic, Indian, Western) is booming in 2026, with couples spending heavily on cinematic coverage. These studios have real clients and real revenue — but most have outdated, low-converting websites with no pricing, no online booking, and no visual hierarchy. Perfect timing as wedding season ramps up Apr–Jun before summer heat kills demand.
