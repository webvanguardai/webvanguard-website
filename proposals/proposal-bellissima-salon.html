What We Found

We reviewed thebellissimasalon.com and identified key areas where a modern redesign would directly impact your bookings and client experience.

Mobile Experience Needs Work

Over 80% of salon clients in Dubai browse on their phones. Your current site wasn't designed mobile-first, which means potential clients are leaving before they book.

📅

No Online Booking

Clients today expect to book instantly. Without a clear booking flow or WhatsApp integration on mobile, you're losing appointments to competitors who make it easy.

No SEO optimization, no schema markup, no meta descriptions. When someone searches "salon Discovery Gardens" — your website doesn't show up where it should.

Outdated Content (Since 2021)

Blog posts with numbered titles, generic descriptions, and no recent updates signal to Google and clients that the site is inactive. Fresh content builds trust.

Design Doesn't Match Your Quality

You offer premium services — balayage, keratin, nail art — but the website doesn't communicate that luxury. First impressions happen online now.
