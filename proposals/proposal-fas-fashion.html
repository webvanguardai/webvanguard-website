The Challenge

What We Found on Your Current Website

After analysing your existing web presence, we identified several opportunities that are costing you customers every day:

🛒

No E-Commerce

Your products are displayed as static images with no way to browse, filter, or purchase. Customers can't shop online — you're losing revenue 24/7.

📸

No Visual Lookbook

Fashion sells through imagery. Your current site shows small, repetitive product cards (Bag, Sandal, Purse) with no editorial photography or lifestyle imagery.

📱

Template Design

A generic WordPress template doesn't reflect 20 years of heritage. Your brand deserves a bespoke, luxury digital experience that matches your in-store quality.

📍

No Store Locator

No map, no directions, no hours. Visitors can't easily find you at Dubai Outlet Mall. A simple Google Maps embed would drive foot traffic.

📲

No Instagram Integration

Your social content isn't connected to your website. An Instagram feed would showcase your latest styles and build social proof automatically.

🔍

Weak SEO

Missing meta tags, no schema markup, no sitemap. You're invisible in "fashion Dubai" or "boutique Dubai Outlet Mall" searches — your competitors aren't.
