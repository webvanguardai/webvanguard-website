What We Found

We analyzed your current website and identified specific areas holding back your online growth:

❌ No visible pricing

Your membership packages (monthly, 2-month, 6-month) redirect to WhatsApp without showing any price. Visitors leave before reaching out — 68% of gym searchers compare prices online before contacting.

❌ No online booking

Every enquiry requires a WhatsApp message. There's no form, no trial booking, no way for a visitor to take action at 11 PM when they're motivated. You're losing leads while you sleep.

❌ No testimonials or social proof

You mention "340 satisfied clients" but there are no real reviews, no names, no stories. Visitors can't trust a number without faces behind it.

❌ No trainer profiles

Your coaches are invisible. In a city with 500+ gyms, people choose trainers — not equipment. Showcasing your team builds trust and justifies premium pricing.

❌ No Google Maps embed

Your address says "Al Mina Road Next to Capital Hotel" but there's no interactive map. Visitors have to copy-paste the address manually — friction that kills walk-ins.

❌ Filler content instead of conversion

The site includes a "Workout for Beginners" tutorial section that reads like a blog post, not a gym website. It dilutes the premium positioning.

What We'd Build

✅ Transparent membership pricing

Clear plans with AED pricing visible above the fold. Each plan links to WhatsApp with pre-filled message for instant conversion.

✅ Online booking form + free trial CTA

A conversion-focused form that captures leads 24/7. Name, phone, preferred program — sent directly to your inbox and WhatsApp.

✅ Real testimonials section

Member stories with names and ratings. Social proof that converts browsers into members.

✅ Coach profiles with credentials

Photos, specializations, certifications. Let visitors choose their coach before they walk in.

✅ Google Maps + SEO optimization

Interactive map, local business schema, and keywords targeting "gym Al Mina Dubai" — so Google sends you free traffic.

✅ Mobile-first, fast, modern design

80%+ of Dubai gym searches happen on mobile. Your new site loads in under 2 seconds and looks premium on every screen.
